Respool: logo and brand

Symbol: a spool unwinding. Ink thread bands over a green base. Wordmark: lowercase, rounded strokes that match the bands.

Colour

Ink#16162a · bands, wordmark
Respool green#2ed3a2 · base, accents
Deep green#0b7a5a · green text on light
Mist#f7f7fa · light background

Symbol, app icon and small sizes

Usage
  • Clear space around the logo: the height of one thread band, all sides.
  • Minimum size: symbol 16 px, full logo 96 px wide.
  • On dark, use the reversed version (white bands, same green base).
  • Don't recolour the base, stretch it, or add effects.
