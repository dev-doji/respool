Watch. Decide. Act.

three jobs, one contract on reactive, two vaults on the chains

  1. 1

    Watch

    The brain subscribes to swap events from the Respool pool on both chains, and to each vault's own events.

  2. 2

    Decide

    A swap that leaves a vault's range asks for a re-centre. Recent swap activity three times higher on one chain asks for a move.

  3. 3

    Act

    Reactive delivers a callback. The vault re-centres its position, or burns tokens here so the same amount can be minted on the other chain.

    What it is not.

    stated up front, so nobody has to guess

    01

    Test tokens

    Moved liquidity is Respool's own test tokens, burned on one chain and minted on the other. There is no real bridge and no real money.

    02

    Scripted traffic

    Every swap in these runs came from my own script, evenly spaced. The move rule has not yet met real trading.

    03

    A move takes seconds

    A burst of trading shorter than a move is over before the liquidity arrives. That bounds what this can chase.

    04

    Clock resolution

    Base Sepolia reports milliseconds. Unichain Sepolia and Lasna report whole seconds, so their times are ±1 s.

    05

    One-sided after a crossing

    When price runs through a position the vault holds one token. A limit position parks it beside the price instead of leaving it idle.

    06

    No baseline yet

    A run with Ethereum Sepolia as the origin chain, to show what a slow origin costs, is still to do.
